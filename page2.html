About the Game

Schools Out is the first game published under Project Mobius Studios. The idea for the game began after a nightmare inspired by playing Silent Hill 2 Remake.

As a fan of both the Silent Hill and Resident Evil series, I wanted to create a survival horror experience that explored psychological horror, trauma, and personal struggles.

Another major inspiration comes from my Blackfeet culture and the history of oppression experienced by Indigenous communities. The game explores themes of generational trauma and how the past can continue to affect people long after the events have occurred.
